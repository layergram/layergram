# Layergram Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces associated with the public Layergram project, including issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, and any other project communication channels.

We want Layergram to be a place where people can collaborate on serious privacy-focused software in a **welcoming, respectful, and professional** environment.

---

## 1. Our Pledge

As contributors and maintainers of Layergram, we pledge to:

- Act and interact in ways that foster an open, inclusive, and healthy community.
- Make participation in the project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.
- Treat each other with respect, especially when we disagree technically.

---

## 2. Our Standards

### 2.1 Positive behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing when we make mistakes, and learning from them.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the wider community, not just for ourselves.
- Helping new contributors feel welcome and supported.

### 2.2 Unacceptable behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information (such as physical or email addresses) without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

---

## 3. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies:

- Within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, chat, and related communication channels).
- When an individual is officially representing the Layergram project in public spaces, for example:
  - Using an official project email address.
  - Posting from an official project or organization account.
  - Presenting the project at an event or in public communications.

---

## 4. Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers and designated community leaders are responsible for:

- Clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior.
- Taking appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.
- Removing, editing, or rejecting comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, or other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct.
- Communicating reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce the Code of Conduct in good faith may be removed from their roles.

---

## 5. Reporting and Enforcement

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct:

1. Prefer private reporting using the contact information provided in the `README.md` or on https://layergram.app.
2. For security-sensitive situations or privacy risks, use **security@layergram.app**.
3. Include, when possible:
   - A description of what happened.
   - Where it happened (issue, pull request, discussion, etc.).
   - Any relevant links or screenshots, if safe to share.

All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
Project leaders will respect the privacy and safety of the reporter to the fullest extent possible.

---

## 6. Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders will use these guidelines to determine the consequences for conduct they deem to be in violation of this Code of Conduct.

### 6.1 Correction

**Community impact:**
Use of inappropriate language or behavior that is unprofessional or unwelcome, but believed to have resulted from carelessness rather than malice.

**Consequence:**
A private, written warning from community leaders, providing clarity around the violation and why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

---

### 6.2 Warning

**Community impact:**
A single serious violation or a series of repeated violations.

**Consequence:**
A warning with clearly stated consequences for continued behavior. The person may be asked to avoid direct interaction with specific individuals or parts of the project for a specified period of time. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

---

### 6.3 Temporary Ban

**Community impact:**
Serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence:**
A temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the project community for a specified period of time. During this period, the person may not interact with the project through issues, pull requests, discussions, or other public channels.

---

### 6.4 Permanent Ban

**Community impact:**
Demonstrating a pattern of violation of community standards, persistent harassment or aggression, or clear refusal to cooperate with reasonable enforcement actions.

**Consequence:**
A permanent ban from any interaction with the project community.

---

## 7. Relationship to Other Project Documents

This Code of Conduct works together with:

- `CONTRIBUTING.md`, which describes how to propose changes and what belongs in the public Layergram repository.
- The project license and any future Contributor License Agreement (CLA), which define legal terms for contributions.

By participating in this project, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct in all project-related spaces.

If you have questions or concerns about this Code of Conduct, please use the official contact information referenced in the `README.md`.
